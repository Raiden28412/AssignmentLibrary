SEIGE AND STORM

Leigh Bardugo

Power rises. Darkness spreads. The fight for the future of Ravka begins.

Description

In Siege and Storm, Alina Starkov flees the Darkling’s grasp, but his newfound power threatens all of Ravka. With the help of the cunning privateer Sturmhond, she seeks to harness her strength and rally an army. As darkness spreads, Alina must decide how much she’s willing to sacrifice to save her world..
