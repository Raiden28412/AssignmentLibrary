Description

Ruin and Rising is the thrilling conclusion to Leigh Bardugo’s Grisha Trilogy. Alina must rise from the shadows to face the Darkling, seek the final amplifier, and save Ravka. With love, sacrifice, and war at stake, her destiny will be decided in an epic battle of light and darkness.
